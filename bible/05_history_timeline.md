# History and Timeline

Dates are *After Kindling* (AK). "BK" counts years before the Kindling.

## The ages

| Age | Span | Summary (public) | Truth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Age of Warmth** | unknown – 0 BK | Myth: the world was born in a golden dawn. | The First Light, a vast benevolent power, warmed Talmerra; spirits, elves and dragons lived in its glow. |
| **The Kindling** | 0 AK | The Eight lit the first lantern and drove back the endless night. | The Eight devoured the First Light and became gods. Its body shattered into the Sundered Planes. |
| **The Age of Lanterns** | 1 – 612 AK | The Lamplighters were founded; roads were built; kingdoms rose. | The Lamplighters began mining First Light embers in the Underdeep as lantern-cores, believing them "god-stones". |
| **The Age of Crowns** | 612 – 1001 AK | Corvane, the Saffron Empire and the dwarven holds became great powers. | The Unlight began to creep further each century as the First Light's embers ran thin. |
| **The Quiet Century** | 1001 – 1047 AK | Peace, trade and printing. | The Dimming began, slowly, in remote places no one counted. |

## Secret history of Oriel's birth

> SECRET (reveal: C9, with hints from C1): Oriel was not abandoned. She was carried to Lantern No. 1 by Ysolde Marr herself, who found an orphan of a burned Lamplighter family on the road and could not bring herself to raise a child in the life she had chosen. Ysolde left the infant with a brass Lamplighter's button that Oriel still wears. The button is engraved "Y.M. — one more mile". Ilsevel recognises the engraving style in C2 but not its owner until C6.

## Secret history of Hilde Ironvow

> SECRET (reveal: C5 end, rescue C6): The Shaft Nine collapse was triggered by Unlit agents digging for First Light embers under Hold Aldhammer. Hilde survived and was carried into the Underdeep, where the aboleth Ulathyx enthralled her. She appears in C6 as the aboleth's voice before she is freed.

## The Gloamkey

> SECRET (reveal: C3E30 partial, C7 full): The Gloamkey is a Lamplighter master key, made by Saint Wenna, that opens any Lamplighter vault — including the Archive Beneath and the Tomb of the First Sultan's lantern-vault. The Velvet Hand were hired by the Unlit to steal it from Lamplighters' Hall in 1041; Tamsin then stole it from the Hand without knowing what it was.
