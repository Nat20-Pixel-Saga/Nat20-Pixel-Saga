# The Eight

The gods of Talmerra are called **the Eight**. They are worshipped together in most temples, each with an alcove; individuals choose a patron. All eight accept prayer at any lantern.

## Personalities and how they act

The gods do not walk the world. They speak through omens, dreams, a flicker of a lantern, the behaviour of animals. From Campaign 6, the party learns the gods can also act through **Exarchs** — chosen mortals given a spark of divine flame.

- **Aurelan** is kind, sincere and, the audience will slowly learn, frightened. She is the god who most depends on the lanterns and the one who most regrets the Kindling.
- **Vigrun** values the oath above the outcome. He would rather a dwarf keep a bad promise than break a good one. Brannoc's arc is a long argument with him.
- **Selvhara** remembers everything and forgives nothing; she is the god who most sympathises with Ysolde.
- **Quill** is playful, generous to the lucky and indifferent to the unlucky. She once stole a secret from the other gods, which is why the Lamplighter archives exist (C6 reveal).
- **Ondrel** is grief itself. She drowned her own domain once in sorrow; storms follow her moods.
- **Kesh** is proud and the most openly hungry for prayer; the Saffron Empire's theocracy feeds him.
- **Morrow** is gentle and honest; he is the only one of the Eight who will tell the party the truth when asked directly (C9).
- **Thessin** never speaks. Their priests take vows of silence and communicate by sign and writing.

## The secret of the Eight

> SECRET (reveal: C6E38 "The Archive Beneath", full confirmation C9): The Eight were once mortal-born spirits who lived in the First Light's warmth. In the Age of Kindling they fed on the First Light, consumed it, and became gods. Its body broke into the Sundered Planes; its dying is the Unlight. Ever since, the Eight have sustained themselves with mortal prayer and with the First Light's last embers, which the Lamplighters unknowingly harvest as lantern-cores.
>
> The Eight are not evil. Most regret what they did. But they will not willingly die, and they guide mortal fates to keep the prayers coming. Ysolde Marr discovered this in the Archive Beneath and concluded the gods are parasites.

## Worship and practice

- **Dawn and dusk devotions** at lanterns: a short spoken line, e.g. "Hold the light, hold the road."
- **Temples** have eight alcoves around a central altar with a Beacon (a great lantern-core). A temple whose Beacon dies is "dark" and cannot perform holy rites until it is re-kindled.
- **Clergy titles:** acolyte → priest/priestess → reverend → hierarch. The church of the Eight is led by the **Conclave of Hierarchs** in Highcrest.
- **Holy days:** Lamplight Festival (21 Highsun), Night of Lanterns (30 Duskwane, when every lantern is visited), each god's feast day.

## Divine magic on screen

- A cleric's spell shows a golden spark at their holy symbol before the effect.
- Each god's magic has a tint: Aurelan gold, Vigrun ember red, Selvhara green, Quill violet, Ondrel blue, Kesh crimson, Morrow pale grey, Thessin indigo.
- Oriel's healing is always gold with a soft chime.
