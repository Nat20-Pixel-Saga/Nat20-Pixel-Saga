# Campaign Arcs

## Campaign 1 — The Lantern Road (levels 1 → 3, 40 episodes)

**Logline:** When the lanterns on the oldest road in the Vael start going dark, four strangers in a frontier inn become the only people willing to walk into the night.

**Themes:** strangers becoming a team; small heroism; what light means to ordinary people.

**Bond goals by the finale:** Brannoc and Oriel trust each other completely; Tamsin admits one true thing about herself; Ilsevel chooses the party over orders.

## Interlude I1 — Oriel's Light (5 episodes)
Oriel's first pilgrimage to the Beacon at Highcrest's outer chapel; she hears a voice in the Beacon that is not Aurelan's. Shopping in a market town; Brannoc buys a better shield.

## Campaign 2 — The Sunken Abbey (levels 3 → 5)

- **Act 1:** Mirefen and Stilthollow; bog bodies walk; the bell that rings itself.
- **Act 2:** Into the drowned abbey; the monks' ghosts relive their last day. **Level 4** (E20).
- **Act 3:** Cinder, the first Wick, arrives to steal the Heartflame; a three-way battle.
- **Act 4:** Prior Edric Vane, the wight abbot, asks to be released. The party chooses to destroy or free the Heartflame. Ilsevel finds a letter in Ysolde's handwriting. **Level 5** (E43): Extra Attack, 3rd-level spells (Fireball arrives on screen).

## Interlude I2 — Tamsin's Debt
A Velvet Hand collector finds Tamsin. She lies to the party; they find out.

## Campaign 3 — The Ember Coast (levels 5 → 7)

- **Act 1:** Port Sable; the Velvet Hand demands the Gloamkey; heist set-up.
- **Act 2:** Heist on Corvina Lisle's vault — or an alliance. **Level 6.**
- **Act 3:** Pirates of the Free Captains; sea travel; the Cinder Isles.
- **Act 4:** The Brine Sisters' grotto; the Unlit attack Ondrel's Lighthouse; the Gloamkey glows near the lighthouse's Lamplighter lock — revealed as a master key. **Level 7.** One of the Lantern Stars goes dark.

## Interlude I3 — Ilsevel's Homecoming
Ilsevel returns to Hollowmere; her family wants her to stay. She chooses the party.

## Campaign 4 — Court of Thorns (levels 7 → 9)

- **Act 1:** The Thornwild; dusk portals; Hollowmere's elves are vanishing into the Gloaming.
- **Act 2:** The Court of Thorns; fey bargains; Tamsin trades a memory. **Level 8.**
- **Act 3:** The Mirror Maze; Thorn, Ilsevel's rival, appears.
- **Act 4:** Lady Sorrelwyn's masquerade; **Ysolde Marr appears in person** and speaks to Ilsevel. She does not fight; she leaves. **Level 9.**

## Interlude I4 — Brannoc's Oath
Brannoc returns to the Iron Peaks' foothills; he receives a summons from his clan.

## Campaign 5 — The Iron Peaks (levels 9 → 11)

- **Act 1:** Hold Aldhammer under siege by fire giants; Brannoc must ask to be let in.
- **Act 2:** Investigation of Shaft Nine; Anvil the Wick. **Level 10.**
- **Act 3:** Frostgate Pass; avalanche; frost giant mercenaries.
- **Act 4:** Mount Skarn; Jarl Brakka Emberjaw's Unlit war engine; duel by giant law. Proof that Hilde was taken alive. **Level 11.**

## Interlude I5 — Whole party: Winter in the Hold
Festival of Hearthdays; romance beats; Brannoc's name restored (or not) to the Book of Debts.

## Campaign 6 — The Underdeep Descent (levels 11 → 13)

- **Act 1:** Descent through Shaft Nine; Lowmarket.
- **Act 2:** The Fungal Cathedral; the Bleaching Hall (**reveal: the Unlight is the First Light's death**). **Level 12.**
- **Act 3:** The Archive Beneath (using the Gloamkey) — **the secret of the Eight**. Ysolde offers alliance; the party argues on screen. Thorn's arc turns.
- **Act 4:** The Still Lake; Ulathyx; Hilde freed. Anvil dies. **Level 13.**

## Interlude I6 — Oriel's Doubt
Oriel's prayers go unanswered for an episode; Father Tobin's letter.

## Campaign 7 — Sands of the Sundered (levels 13 → 15)

- **Act 1:** Across the Saffron Sea; Zahrun; licences and intrigue.
- **Act 2:** The Glass Wastes; genies; Cinder's redemption choice. **Level 14.**
- **Act 3:** The Palace of Ten Thousand Lamps; Vizier Ombrasel's court.
- **Act 4:** The Tomb of the First Sultan; the phylactery in the Gloamkey vault; Ysolde's plan revealed in her own words. **Level 15.**

## Interlude I7 — Tamsin's Name
Tamsin decides whether to keep or return the Gloamkey.

## Campaign 8 — Storm Over the Capital (levels 15 → 17)

- **Act 1:** Highcrest; lanterns failing across the kingdom; refugees.
- **Act 2:** The Conclave's cover-up; Aldous Venn's confession; Ash unmasked as Corin Venn. **Level 16.**
- **Act 3:** Vorthaxis Stormcrown attacks; the Free Captains' fleet; Corvina's guild.
- **Act 4:** Thunderhead Spire; the dragon falls; Ysolde takes the Great Beacon's flame and ascends partly. **Level 17.**

## Interlude I8 — Ilsevel and the Last Letter
Ilsevel reads Ysolde's final letter; the truth of Oriel's button begins to emerge.

## Campaign 9 — The Shattered Planes (levels 17 → 19)

- **Act 1:** The Ashen Fields; meeting the dead; Morrow tells the truth.
- **Act 2:** The Ribcage Cathedral; **Oriel's origin revealed**. **Level 18.**
- **Act 3:** The Lower Furnace; General Malcarrow's flame market.
- **Act 4:** The Heartstone; the last ember of the First Light chooses Oriel. **Level 19.**

## Interlude I9 — The Last Night
The whole party, one last quiet night before the end. Each pair gets a scene.

## Campaign 10 — Twilight of the Gods (levels 19 → 20)

- **Act 1:** The world goes dark; the Last Lantern at the world's edge.
- **Act 2:** The Hearth of the Eight; the gods plead their case; Caldrazzar the Last Gold.
- **Act 3:** Ysolde's ascension; the party's choice is put to a vote on screen.
- **Act 4:** Final battle and the new dawn. **Level 20** in the final episode. Epilogue: the wiki's last page is written in the narrator's voice.
