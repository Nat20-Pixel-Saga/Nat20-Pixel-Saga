# People

- **[Father Tobin](tobin-ashdown.md)** - Mentioned only: he packed the shrine bread that Oriel brought for the goblin.
- **[Maddy Fenn](maddy-fenn.md)** - Innkeeper of the Crooked Kettle. Counted her broken shutters, scolded Brannoc for sitting up all night, and offered free board and hot meals to whoever brings back the light of Lantern 37.
- **[Rusk](rusk.md)** - The small goblin taken alive in the raid on Brindle Cross. Spent the night tied to the Crooked Kettle's rain barrel, then talked after Oriel fed him. Speaks in short, broken sentences and calls himself by name. Now kept in the Kettle's cellar with a blanket, asleep on Maddy's onions.
- **[Wil Fenn](wil-fenn.md)** - Maddy's son. Asked to go with the party to Lantern 37; Brannoc told him to stay and hold the village, which seemed to please him.
