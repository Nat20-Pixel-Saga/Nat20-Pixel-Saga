# People

- **[Father Tobin Ashdown](tobin-ashdown.md)** - Old priest of the Shrine of the Open Hand who raised Oriel. Gave her his own sun-disc holy symbol after her first dusk devotion; admits he never understood half the verses.
- **[Maddy Fenn](maddy-fenn.md)** - Innkeeper of the Crooked Kettle. Brisk and warm; keeps the Lamplighters' ledger for the lanterns south of the village and asks who is paying for everything.
- **[Wil Fenn](wil-fenn.md)** - Maddy Fenn's son, sixteen. Lost his coppers to Tamsin at cards and wants to join the soldiers at Fort Harrow; offered Brannoc his services with a pike he has practised with a broom.
