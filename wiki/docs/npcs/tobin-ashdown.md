# Father Tobin

Old priest of Aurelan at the Shrine of the Open Hand, who walks with a cane and raised Oriel. Found her as a baby at the foot of Lantern No. 1 on a frost night in 1022 and named her Oriel, 'golden'. Tells the story of Saint Wenna well, admits he doesn't understand half the scripture, and fusses over whether Oriel has eaten. Keeps the party's three dead lantern-cores on the shrine altar.
