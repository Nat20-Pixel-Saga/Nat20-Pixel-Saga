# The Lantern Road

The old road south from Brindle Cross toward Fort Harrow, one lantern to every mile. A milestone a mile out reads 'Brindle Cross 1 mile, Fort Harrow 39'. Around dead Lantern 37 no birds sing and the grass has turned grey.
