# Brindle Cross

Crossroads village whose south road brings it flour, letters and doctors. Nobody in the village knows how to light a lantern.

*First seen: [C01-E001](../episodes/c01-e001.md)*

## Over time

- *C01-E001*: Crossroads village at the start of the Lantern Road, built around a market square with a well, an inn, a shrine and a smithy.
- *C01-E002*: Crossroads village whose south road brings it flour, letters and doctors. Nobody in the village knows how to light a lantern.
