# C1E5 - The Scout Camp

*In-world date: 4 Emberfall 1047 AK*  
*Seed: `C01-E005-1`*

[Download the episode video](https://github.com/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/releases/tag/C01-E005)

## Summary

Later on the night of 4 Emberfall the party found a goblin scout camp east along the trail. With Ilsevel hurt and no spells or prayers left but cantrips, Tamsin walked into the firelight alone and claimed the boss had sent her to collect the stones early; the goblins believed her long enough to let slip that the cart leaves at dawn and the boss counts every stone. When a goblin spotted Brannoc, the party took the camp in two rounds. The cart held three lantern-cores, all dead and cold, and the party took them to look for a safer place to sleep.

## What happened

- The party crept unseen to the edge of a goblin scout camp east of the birch clearing.
- Tamsin walked in alone and convinced the goblins the boss had sent her to collect the stones early; they let slip that the cart leaves at dawn and the boss counts every stone.
- A goblin spotted Brannoc in the bushes and the fight began; Tamsin shot first.
- Brannoc killed a goblin runt and the goblin leader; Oriel's Sacred Flame finished the other runt.
- The goblin leader wounded Tamsin badly; she drank a healing potion mid-fight.
- The cart held three dead lantern-cores, cloudy and cold. Oriel could not bring herself to touch them.
- The party took the cores and headed for a sheltered hollow Pell had spotted south of the fork.
