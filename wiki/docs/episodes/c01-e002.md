# C1E2 - The Goblin Who Talked

*In-world date: 4 Emberfall 1047 AK*  
*Seed: `C01-E002-1`*

[Download the episode video](https://github.com/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/releases/tag/C01-E002)

## Summary

On the morning of 4 Emberfall, Oriel fed the captured goblin while Brannoc stood silently behind her, and the goblin, Rusk, talked: goblins carry the 'shiny stones' from the lanterns up into the hills to their boss and are paid in sacks of meal. Ilsevel judged that he was telling the truth and was afraid of going back. By the well, Ilsevel did not believe Tamsin's story about the velvet thread on her wrist. At noon Maddy Fenn offered free board to whoever brings the light back to Lantern 37, and all four agreed.

## What happened

- Brannoc kept watch over the captured goblin all night outside the Crooked Kettle.
- Oriel knelt with shrine bread while Brannoc stood behind her; her persuasion worked and the goblin gave his name, Rusk.
- Rusk said goblins carry the 'shiny stones' from the lanterns up into the hills to their boss, who pays them in sacks of meal that come from somewhere else.
- Ilsevel read Rusk's face correctly: he was telling the truth and was more afraid of going back than of the party.
- By the well, Ilsevel asked about the velvet thread on Tamsin's wrist; Tamsin's lucky-charm story failed to convince her, and Tamsin changed the subject.
- Rusk was moved to the Kettle's cellar with a blanket.
- Maddy Fenn offered free board and meals to whoever brings back the light of Lantern 37; Brannoc told Wil to stay and hold the village.
- Ilsevel, Oriel, Tamsin and Brannoc all agreed to go, each for a reason of their own.
