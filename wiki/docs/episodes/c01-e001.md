## Summary

On 3 Emberfall 1047 AK, four strangers crossed paths in Brindle Cross: Tamsin won Wil Fenn's coppers at cards, Ilsevel found two odd entries for Lantern 37 in the inn's ledger, and Brannoc met Oriel at the shrine as Father Tobin gave her his holy symbol. Then Lantern 37 went dark and goblins raided the square. The four fought them off together; Ilsevel fell and was brought back by Oriel, and one goblin was taken alive.

## What happened

- Tamsin palmed the Crown card and won Wil Fenn's coppers without him noticing, then gave him a honey cake.
- Ilsevel found that Lantern 37 was trimmed twice in nine days, under a warden's mark she did not recognise.
- Father Tobin gave Oriel his sun-disc holy symbol after her first dusk devotion; Brannoc left silver in the alms box.
- Lantern 37 on the south road went dark; Oriel felt it 'like a candle going out in my chest'.
- Brannoc did not spot the goblins in time, and four of them reached the square.
- The party won in three rounds: Brannoc felled three goblins, Tamsin brought down the last one alive.
- Ilsevel was knocked unconscious by thrown daggers; Oriel's Healing Word brought her back.
- Oriel bandaged the captured goblin; he lived, but only just. Maddy Fenn took everyone in for stew.
