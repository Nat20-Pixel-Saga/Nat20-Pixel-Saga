## Summary

In the small hours of 5 Emberfall the party made its first camp together in a hollow south of the goblin trail, beside an old stone Ilsevel identified as a Lamplighter way-marker from the road that ran there before the Lantern Road. Oriel suggested trying to rekindle the dead cores at Father Tobin's altar. When Tamsin sat against the way-marker, the iron key at her throat hummed and she moved away without a word. Tamsin read Ilsevel's notebook while the elf was in trance and put it back with a sprig of heather. On first watch Oriel saw an anvil under the grey paint of Brannoc's shield, and Brannoc, seeing that she fears the dark, gave her his lantern.

## What happened

- The party made camp in a sheltered hollow and lit a fire in an old stone ring.
- Ilsevel read the moss-covered stone in the hollow: a Lamplighter way-marker from the old road.
- Ilsevel explained that dead cores are rekindled at a temple altar by a senior Lamplighter; Oriel suggested Father Tobin's altar.
- When Tamsin sat against the way-marker, the plain iron key at her neck hummed; she moved away and told Oriel it was smoke in her eyes.
- Tamsin lifted Ilsevel's notebook unnoticed, read Ilsevel's notes about her, and put it back with a sprig of heather.
- On first watch Oriel noticed an anvil shape under the grey paint of Brannoc's shield; he said it was old paint.
- Brannoc saw that Oriel fears the dark and gave her his lantern.
- The party woke rested on the morning of 5 Emberfall.
