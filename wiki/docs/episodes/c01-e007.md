# C1E7 - The Saint of the Open Hand

*In-world date: 5 Emberfall 1047 AK*  
*Seed: `C01-E007-1`*

[Download the episode video](https://github.com/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/releases/tag/C01-E007)

## Summary

On 5 Emberfall the party came back to Brindle Cross with three dead lantern-cores. That evening at the Crooked Kettle, Father Tobin told the story of Saint Wenna of the Open Hand and of the frost night in 1022 when he found a baby at the foot of Lantern No. 1, wrapped in a grey coat with a brass button: Oriel. Ilsevel could recall only the Hall primer's single line on Wenna, and Oriel, praying over a dead core, felt nothing at all. Then Wil brought news that Lantern 41 had gone dark, and a letter from Captain Rook asking 'Bran Vow' to look in on Wending Mill, where goblins have been seen.

## What happened

- The party returned to Brindle Cross with three dead lantern-cores; Maddy kept her promise of free board anyway.
- Father Tobin recognised Brannoc as the man who leaves silver in the shrine's alms box.
- By the Kettle's hearth Tobin told the story of Saint Wenna, who walked the first dark road with a lantern in her open hand.
- Ilsevel's attempt to add the Order's history produced only one line from the Hall's primer.
- Oriel prayed over a dead core with Tobin and felt nothing at all; the cores are to be kept on the shrine altar, and Ilsevel will write to Lamplighters' Hall.
- Tobin told how he found baby Oriel at the foot of Lantern No. 1 in 1022, in a grey coat with a brass button.
- Wil reported that Lantern 41 has gone dark.
- A Fort Harrow rider's letter from Captain Rook asked 'Bran Vow' to look in on Wending Mill; Tamsin noticed the name, and Brannoc said it was his army name.
- The party agreed to set out for Wending Mill at dawn.
