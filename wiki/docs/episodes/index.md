# Episodes

| Episode | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| [C1E1 - Lantern Thirty-Seven](c01-e001.md) | On 3 Emberfall 1047 AK, four strangers crossed paths in Brindle Cross: Tamsin won Wil Fenn's coppers at cards, Ilsevel found two odd entries for Lantern 37 in the inn's ledger, and Brannoc met Oriel at the shrine as Father Tobin gave her his holy symbol. Then Lantern 37 went dark and goblins raided the square. The four fought them off together; Ilsevel fell and was brought back by Oriel, and one goblin was taken alive. |
| [C1E2 - The Goblin Who Talked](c01-e002.md) | On the morning of 4 Emberfall, Oriel fed the captured goblin while Brannoc stood silently behind her, and the goblin, Rusk, talked: goblins carry the 'shiny stones' from the lanterns up into the hills to their boss and are paid in sacks of meal. Ilsevel judged that he was telling the truth and was afraid of going back. By the well, Ilsevel did not believe Tamsin's story about the velvet thread on her wrist. At noon Maddy Fenn offered free board to whoever brings the light back to Lantern 37, and all four agreed. |
| [C1E3 - The Empty Housing](c01-e003.md) | On the afternoon of 4 Emberfall the four walked the mile south to Lantern 37, where the grass had gone grey and the glass was frosted in daylight. Tamsin opened the frozen housing catch with a hairpin and noticed fresh scratches: someone had opened it recently without a key. Ilsevel found the core had been lifted out whole, not broken, but could tell nothing about who took it. At dusk Brannoc could not read how many goblins had used the trail east, and the party followed it into Thornpike Woods anyway. |
