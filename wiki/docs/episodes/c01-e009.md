# C1E9 - Smoke Over the Millpond

*In-world date: 6 Emberfall 1047 AK*  
*Seed: `C01-E009-1`*

[Download the episode video](https://github.com/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/Nat20-Pixel-Saga/releases/tag/C01-E009)

## Summary

At Wending Mill on 6 Emberfall, Brannoc heaved the bar off the burning barn's doors and Bet Hollis and a hired lad came out; the lad had breathed too much smoke, and Oriel was still tending him when a goblin boss with lantern-glass riveted to his helmet walked out of the smoke with two of his crew. With no spells left but cantrips, the party fought: the boss cut Oriel down, Brannoc killed the goblin and then held the boss alone while Oriel fought her way back to stable, and Ilsevel's fire bolt finally dropped the boss. Brannoc tended Oriel himself. The barn burned, but the mill was saved.

## What happened

- Brannoc heaved the heat-swollen pole off the burning barn's doors at the first try.
- Bet Hollis walked out; the hired lad had breathed too much smoke, and Oriel could not get him on his feet, so she carried him to the yard's edge.
- A goblin boss in a dented helmet with lantern-glass riveted to it came out of the smoke with two goblins; Ilsevel translated: 'The boss pays for stones. He pays more for the ones who stop us.'
- The boss went straight for Oriel and cut her down; she made her death saves and became stable on her own.
- Ilsevel killed the goblin runt, and Brannoc killed the goblin and then held the boss alone, dropping to his last few hit points.
- Tamsin's arrows all missed the boss; Ilsevel's fire bolt killed him.
- Brannoc tended the unconscious Oriel himself. The barn burned down; the mill was saved.
