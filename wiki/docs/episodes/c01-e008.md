## Summary

On 6 Emberfall the party walked south to answer Captain Rook's letter. Pell spotted goblins battering the door of the watermill at Wending Mill, and the four ran the last stretch and fought four goblins in the mill yard; Brannoc and Tamsin were both badly cut before Tamsin's blade and Ilsevel's spells ended it. The miller, Ned Hollis, said the goblins wanted the grain because 'the boss wants meal'. Then they saw smoke: the barn was burning, barred from outside, with Ned's wife Bet and a hired lad inside, and something behind the smoke had stopped shouting.

## What happened

- On the road south Pell spotted four goblins battering the door of the watermill at Wending Mill.
- The party ran the last stretch and reached the mill yard; Ilsevel and Oriel kept the pace while Brannoc and Tamsin lagged.
- Two goblins won the first moves; Brannoc was cut and twice caught his second wind, and Tamsin was dropped to her last breaths before Oriel's Healing Word.
- Tamsin killed two goblins with sneak attacks; Ilsevel killed the runt with Fire Bolt and the last goblin with Magic Missile.
- The miller, Ned Hollis, said the goblins came for the grain because 'the boss wants meal'.
- The barn was burning, its doors barred from outside, with Ned's wife Bet and a hired lad inside.
- A deeper voice had been giving orders behind the smoke; it stopped as the party reached the barn.
