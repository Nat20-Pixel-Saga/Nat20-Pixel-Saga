## Summary

On the night of 4 Emberfall the goblin trail forked at a great stump in Thornpike Woods. Ilsevel performed an hour-long ritual and called her familiar for the first time: a small brown owl she named Pell. In the birch clearing to the north, seeing through Pell's eyes proved dizzying and three wolves were on the party before anyone saw them; Tamsin went down to a wolf's bite until Oriel's prayer brought her back, and the party killed all three. Ilsevel found the wolves starved and grey at the fur tips, like the grass at Lantern 37, and the party set off east.

## What happened

- At the fork in the goblin trail, Brannoc kept watch and counted trees while Ilsevel performed an hour-long ritual.
- Ilsevel called her familiar for the first time, a small brown owl, and named him Pell.
- In the birch clearing, Ilsevel's first try at seeing through Pell's eyes failed: three wolves reached the party before anyone saw them.
- Ilsevel's Shield turned two bites; a wolf's bite dropped Tamsin, and Oriel's Healing Word brought her back.
- Ilsevel killed one wolf with Shocking Grasp and Brannoc killed the other two.
- Ilsevel found the wolves starved, far from their range, and grey at the fur tips, like the grass at Lantern 37.
- Pell returned to Ilsevel, and the party set off east along the trail.
