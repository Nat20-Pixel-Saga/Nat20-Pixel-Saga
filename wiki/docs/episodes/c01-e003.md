## Summary

On the afternoon of 4 Emberfall the four walked the mile south to Lantern 37, where the grass had gone grey and the glass was frosted in daylight. Tamsin opened the frozen housing catch with a hairpin and noticed fresh scratches: someone had opened it recently without a key. Ilsevel found the core had been lifted out whole, not broken, but could tell nothing about who took it. At dusk Brannoc could not read how many goblins had used the trail east, and the party followed it into Thornpike Woods anyway.

## What happened

- Brannoc asked Tamsin why a card player would walk an empty road; she answered with a tall tale.
- Ilsevel read the milestone and declared the carving half a mile out.
- Near Lantern 37 the birds had fallen silent and the grass had gone grey; Ilsevel called it fringe Unlight, arriving far faster than her books say it should.
- Tamsin worked the frost-stiff housing catch open with a hairpin, just barely, and pointed out fresh scratches: someone had opened it lately without a key.
- Ilsevel's examination failed to show who took the core or how, but it was clear the core had been lifted out whole rather than broken.
- Oriel laid her hand on the cold post and prayed; she told Brannoc it was only cold.
- Brannoc could not read the goblin trail: he could not tell how many goblins had used it or when.
- At dusk the party followed the trail east into Thornpike Woods, Brannoc in front.
