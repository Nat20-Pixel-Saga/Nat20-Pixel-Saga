## Appearance

Small, quick, with a round freckled face, dark curly hair under a violet hood, and a wide smile that she uses like a weapon. A thin velvet thread is tied around her left wrist — the mark of the Velvet Hand — which she claims is "a lucky charm from my grandmother". Wears dark leather armour with too many pockets, two daggers she rarely uses, a shortsword she uses a lot, and a shortbow. Around her neck on a cord: a small, plain iron key she never takes off. (It is the **Gloamkey**.)

**Sprite notes:** smallest party sprite; violet cloak and scarf stream when she runs; idle animation is flipping a coin.

## Backstory

Tamsin grew up in the stilt-houses of Port Sable's Underbough quarter, the middle of seven children of a net-mender. Her little brother **Pip** was sickly; medicine cost money; Tamsin was good with her hands. By fourteen she was a runner for the **Velvet Hand**, by twenty a full member with the velvet thread, by twenty-five the guild's best "lift" artist.

In 1042 AK the guild sent her into its own deep vault to fetch payment for a client. Inside she found a small iron key on a velvet cushion that sang when she touched it — a sound only she could hear. She didn't know why, but she knew she couldn't leave it there. She took it, sold her share of the job to a fence for Pip's medicine, and ran. The Hand wants the key back or 2,000 gold crowns. She has been running for five years: river barges, travelling fairs, fake names. Pip, now 22 and healthy, works as a clerk in Highcrest and thinks his sister is a successful merchant.

When the series begins she is playing cards at the Crooked Kettle under the name "Tam Mossfoot", winning slightly too much.

## Personality

- **Traits:** Gives everyone a nickname within an hour (Brannoc is "Big Man", Ilsevel is "Professor", Oriel is "Sunshine"). Can't resist a locked box. Tells outrageous stories that are 30% true.
- **Ideal:** *Freedom.* "Nobody owns anybody. Not guilds, not kings, not gods."
- **Bond:** Pip. And, increasingly, the first people who ever saw through her and stayed.
- **Flaw:** Lies by reflex, especially about things that matter; would rather run than be pinned down.
- **Fears:** being caged; being found out as "just a thief".
- **Loves:** shiny things, honey cakes, card games, being underestimated, Pell (secretly).
- **Dislikes:** guilds, sermons, being picked up (Brannoc did it once), the smell of the sea (it means home).

## Growth arc

- **Tier 1:** a liar on the run who finds a reason to stay.
- **Tier 2:** must face the guild and her past; stops running.
- **Tier 3:** learns the key matters to the whole world; chooses responsibility over freedom, once.
- **Tier 4:** Quill offers her the gods' own trick — to steal fate itself. In C10 she steals the moment that makes the third path possible.
