## Appearance

Medium height, sturdy from years of hauling water and lantern-oil, short auburn hair she cuts herself, freckles everywhere. Wears second-hand chain mail under a white tabard sewn with a gold Open Hand, a shield painted with a lantern, and a heavy warhammer that used to belong to a shrine guard. On a cord with her holy symbol hangs a **brass Lamplighter's button** — the only thing found with her as a baby.

**Sprite notes:** white tabard (colour 6) is the brightest thing on screen in dark scenes; holy symbol glints gold when she casts; idle animation is touching the button.

## Backstory

On a frost night in 1022 AK a baby was found at the foot of **Lantern No. 1** in Brindle Cross, wrapped in a grey coat with a brass button. Father Tobin Ashdown of the Shrine of the Open Hand took her in and named her Oriel, "golden", after the lantern-light she was found in. She grew up in the shrine, lighting the dusk devotions, nursing the sick, learning hymns and the shrine guard's hammer drills.

Oriel's faith is real and uncomplicated: Aurelan keeps the light, and the light keeps people safe. She has never left the Lanternmarch. She has felt the goddess answer her prayers since she was twelve — a warmth in her chest, "like someone holding a lamp behind me".

She is not naive. She has buried villagers, sat with the dying and talked a drunk husband out of hurting his wife. She simply believes kindness is stronger than the alternative and is willing to bet her life on it.

When Lantern 37 dies on the first night of the series, she feels the warmth in her chest flicker for the first time.

## Personality

- **Traits:** Asks everyone their name and remembers it. Sings when she is nervous (badly, enthusiastically). Feeds everyone. Kneels to talk to children and goblins alike.
- **Ideal:** *Mercy.* "Everyone gets one more chance. Some people need two."
- **Bond:** Father Tobin and Brindle Cross. The button and the question of who left it.
- **Flaw:** Cannot bear to give up on anyone, including people who will hurt her friends; puts herself between enemies and the people she's trying to save.
- **Fears:** the warmth going out; being the reason someone dies because she trusted the wrong person.
- **Loves:** dawn, hymns, bread, stray animals, the moment a fever breaks.
- **Dislikes:** cruelty, waste, people who use the gods as excuses.

## Growth arc

- **Tier 1:** a village priestess who has never left home; learns the world is larger and her faith works anywhere.
- **Tier 2:** the warmth weakens; she learns to act on faith without feeling it.
- **Tier 3:** learns the gods' secret; faith in the gods breaks; faith in people takes its place.
- **Tier 4:** chosen by the last ember of the First Light; becomes the bearer of a new, shared light — not a god's, the world's.
