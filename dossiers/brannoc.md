## Appearance

Broad as a door, short even for a dwarf, with a copper-red beard in two plaits bound with iron rings — he has cut off the clan ring that should sit at the end of each. A long scar runs from his left ear into the beard. Wears battered royal-army chain mail with the Corvane crest scraped off, a red cape that has seen better decades, and a tall rounded shield painted grey over an older design. When he thinks no one is looking he traces the painted-over shape with his thumb: an anvil.

**Sprite notes:** wide silhouette, shield always on the left arm, cape (colour 19) moves on the walk cycle; idle animation is him adjusting the shield strap.

## Backstory

Brannoc was born in Hold Aldhammer, the elder of two children of a mine-warden. He and his sister **Hilde** were inseparable: she was the loud one, he was the careful one. At sixty he became a warden himself, responsible for the deep shafts.

In 1039 AK an engineer named **Dorrek Stoneback** asked for Shaft Nine to be opened past the old seal, claiming a new silver vein. Brannoc signed the order. Three days later Shaft Nine collapsed with Hilde and eleven miners inside. No bodies were recovered. The Thane's court ruled that Brannoc had signed an unsafe order; his name was struck from the **Book of Debts**, the clan's honour ledger, and he was exiled "until the debt of Shaft Nine is paid". Nobody told him how such a debt could be paid.

He walked south, enlisted in the Corvane royal army under a shortened name ("Bran Vow"), and served three years at Fort Harrow under Captain Edda Rook, who knew exactly who he was and never said so. He is good at the work: holding a line, keeping younger soldiers alive. He has been offered a sergeant's stripes twice and refused both times.

The series opens with him carrying dispatches from Fort Harrow to the Lamplighters, stopping for one night at the Crooked Kettle.

## Personality

- **Traits:** Says little and means all of it. Counts things when anxious (exits, enemies, arrows). Takes the first watch and the worst bunk without discussion.
- **Ideal:** *Duty.* "You keep the line so others don't have to."
- **Bond:** Hilde. Every decision is measured against whether she would have been proud.
- **Flaw:** He thinks he deserves to be hurt, so he takes risks with his own life that frighten his friends — and he cannot accept praise or comfort.
- **Fears:** enclosed collapsing spaces (disadvantage on nothing mechanically; show it in narration), being responsible for another death.
- **Loves:** stout, whittling small animals, children who aren't scared of him, the moment before a fight when everything is simple.
- **Dislikes:** speeches, nobles, wasted food, people who joke about the dead.

- He knows the name of the engineer (Dorrek Stoneback) and has never said it aloud. When he hears "Stoneback" again (C5) it is a major moment.
- He sends half his army pay every month to the families of the eleven miners, anonymously, through a Lamplighter courier. (Revealed by Tamsin, who notices, in C2.)

## Growth arc

- **Tier 1 (L1–4):** a soldier hiding inside a uniform; learns to follow the party rather than orders.
- **Tier 2 (L5–10):** must face Hold Aldhammer; learns the collapse was not his fault — and that he must forgive himself anyway.
- **Tier 3 (L11–16):** rescues Hilde; must let her lead; becomes the party's shield in the war.
- **Tier 4 (L17–20):** asked by Vigrun to keep a terrible oath; chooses the party's third path, breaking the oath for a better one. "An oath's an oath. Unless it's the wrong one."
